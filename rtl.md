title: Right-to-left languages
permalink: /rtl/
lead: Forma supports right-to-left languages, such as Arabic and Hebrew.
---

1. Go to **Configuration → Languages** and edit the language.
2. Tick **Right-to-left**.
3. Save.

The storefront layout mirrors automatically for that language.

<div class="callout">
<p>A language you add later starts without translated strings for nopCommerce and for Forma. Import its language pack under <strong>Configuration → Languages</strong>.</p>
</div>

Home page section texts are translated in the section form: see [Languages]({{ '/home-page/#languages' | relative_url }}).
