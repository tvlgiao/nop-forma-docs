## How to get support

1. Check this documentation and the [FAQ]({{ '/faq/' | relative_url }}). Many questions are answered there.
2. If you still need help, contact PapaThemes through the contact page on our website.

<p><a class="button" href="{{ site.contact_url }}">Contact PapaThemes</a></p>

## What to include

Include these details so we can help on the first reply:

- Your store URL.
- Your **nopCommerce version** and **Forma version**. Both are shown under **Configuration → Local plugins** (the version of *Forma theme core*).
- What you expected, what happened, and the steps or page where we can see it.
- A screenshot or a short screen recording.

<div class="callout callout--warn">
<p><strong>Never send passwords.</strong> Don't include your admin password or login in a message.</p>
</div>
