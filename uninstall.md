title: Uninstall
permalink: /uninstall/
lead: How to remove Forma from your store.
---

1. Go to **Configuration → Settings → General settings** and select another theme under *Store theme*. Save.
2. Go to **Configuration → Local plugins**, click **Uninstall** next to *Forma theme core*, restart the application, then click **Delete**.
3. Delete the `Themes/Forma` folder if you no longer need the theme.

<div class="callout callout--warn">
<p>Uninstalling the plugin removes Forma's settings and texts, including your home page sections. Installing it again starts from the default preset.</p>
</div>
